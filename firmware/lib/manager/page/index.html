Up next

Queue tracks for the next home run, the next Mets win, or a specific player’s next home run. When a queue runs out, the Apple picks at random again. Add to the end here, or tap Play next on any track in the library to put it first.

Add a track

Pick a file, keep the part you want, name it, and choose what it plays for.

Choose the part to keep
Start 0:00Keeps 0:00End 0:00

MP3, M4A, WAV and most others. Up to 30 MB. Drag the handles to keep up to 7 minutes of it. A home run plays the start of what you keep, for as long as the Apple stays up; a win can play all of it.

Preview plays a track here, on this device. Play on the Apple plays it through the Apple's own speaker, at the volume set in Settings.

Library

Choose which tracks play for a home run and which play when the Mets win. Any track can be lined up to play next.

Player tracks

Give a player tracks of his own. When he hits a home run, one of his tracks plays instead of the general home run tracks. Players without their own tracks use the general ones.
